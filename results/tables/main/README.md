# Content #

- `table1_idcatalogue.csv`: list of all birds that are used in analysis including individual id, session ids, species and colony characteristics
- `table2_budgets_species.csv`: Table with species-specific activity and energy budgets
- `table3_budgets_population.csv`: Table with population-specific activity and energy budgets
- `table4_budgets_individual.csv`: Table with individual-specific activity and energy budgets
- `table5_migratory_distance.csv`: Table with individual migratory distance
- `table6_totalNBCosts.csv`: Table with individual WEE_cov_nb and TEE_nb
- `table7_stats_WEE_vs_TEE.csv`: Table with results of stats investigating relationship between WEE_cov_nb and TEE_nb
- `table8_stats_WEE_vs_pred.csv`: Table with results of stats investigating effects of various predictors on WEE_cov_nb
- `table9_stats_TEE_vs_pred.csv`: Table with results of stats investigating effects of various predictors on TEE_nb
